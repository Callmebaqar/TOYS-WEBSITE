Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

We offer standard delivery within 5-7 business days across Pakistan. For orders placed in Lahore, delivery may arrive within 2-3 business days. Express delivery options are also available at checkout for an additional fee.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused, unopened items in their original packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Please note that opened or used items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

Are your toys safe for children?

Absolutely! Safety is our top priority. All toys sold on ToyVerse are sourced from trusted manufacturers and meet international safety standards. Our toys are tested for harmful substances, sharp edges, and small parts. We only stock age-appropriate products with clear age recommendations on every listing.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with your tracking number. You can use this tracking number on our website or the courier partner's site to track your package in real-time. You can also log in to your ToyVerse account to view all your order details and tracking information.
